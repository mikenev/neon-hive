# Neon Hive

A neon tube shooter made in Unity 6 (WebGL). Your ship circles the inside of a curving tunnel and blasts the bugs swarming toward you, through three themes (Ion Drift, Acid Hive and Redline Core), 12 levels and 3 bosses.

## Controls

| | Steer | Fire | Pause / resume | Menus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keyboard** | Left / Right arrows or A / D | Space (hold) | Esc | Up / Down (or W / S), then Space or Enter |
| **Gamepad** | D-pad or left stick | A or right trigger (hold) | Start | D-pad up / down, then A |
| **Touch** | Drag left / right | Hold a finger down (auto-fire) | Pause button (top right); tap to resume | Tap a button |

The mouse works on menus too: click a button.

## Originality

Neon Hive is inspired by classic tube shooters. All of its code, art, music and sound effects are original and were made for this project.
